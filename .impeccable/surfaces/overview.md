# Bouncer Overview

## Direction contract

THESIS: The overview answers “what needs attention?” first and opens the evidence behind suspicious sessions without making the operator decode a grid of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Keep Bouncer’s dark forest-green surfaces, lime selection and existing verdict palette. Use compact operational context, precise evidence rows and calm glass surfaces. Avoid decorative metaphors that compete with session evidence.

STORY: The operator scans real volume and verdict counts, opens a suspicious session from the prioritized attention queue, then reviews its evidence alongside the traffic trend and known-human honesty metrics. Synthetic data stays labelled. The detector’s block recommendation never appears as an enforced block.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Preserve the app shell and sidebar. Put a compact status rail first, then a ranked, clickable suspicious-session list. Follow it with the time trend and latest activity; retain known-human honesty metrics in the supporting section. On narrow widths, keep the queue first and clear of fixed navigation.

FORM: Evidence-led operational queue — use the existing forest glass system and let real session evidence carry the visual focus.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
