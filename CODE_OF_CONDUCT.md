# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Short version: be decent, assume good intent, critique the code not the person, and make space for people who are learning. Harassment, exclusionary jokes and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report concerns to the team lead or the event organizers. Reports are handled confidentially.
