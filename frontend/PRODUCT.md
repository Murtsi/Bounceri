# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operators investigating suspicious online activity, and public demo visitors inspecting sanitised evidence. A landing page explaining the product is the default home for judges and first-time visitors (decision 080, needs-ack); "Open dashboard" leads to the owner overview at `?tab=overview`. Session, campaign and metric views retain their navigation. Investigations is an additional opt-in tab.

## Product purpose

Bounceri is an online investigation platform. Clean the traffic is its first module. Cases connect an immutable evidence snapshot to the local detector and separate optional bounded-decision and final advisory assessments. Providers and models are shown from configuration and historical assessment metadata. Other modules are explicitly unimplemented.

## Design principles

- Evidence and provenance precede interpretation.
- A synthetic example is always labelled; unknown origin is never called real.
- Missing findings, unavailable services and failed analysis are different states.
- Public visitors inspect records. Administrators control paid review and enforcement outside the public interface.
- Preserve the latest dashboard header, Bouncer logo, splash/motion, owner overview, shadcn/ui navigation and all existing views. The owner-selected PR24 UI is dark-only with glass surfaces and lime accents; preserve the removed theme toggle. Within the additional tab, use the dashboard theme tokens and glass surfaces, blue links and amber synthetic or unavailable states, the existing sans family and monospace identifiers.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Use semantic headings and buttons, visible focus, text as well as colour for statuses, reduced motion and a single-column narrow-screen layout. Keep data records readable without requiring horizontal page scrolling.

This scoped context records the owner-approved implementation plan v3 (2026-11-14); it does not add new product scope.
